# Camillo scan: where the ground data and the price disagree (Oct 11 2026)

**Question:** where does dated, public-but-overlooked evidence about what customers are doing
disagree with the stock price and with consensus, ahead of the next report?

**How:**
- Five research passes covered restaurants, apparel/footwear/toys, apps, leisure/travel, and an
  open "what are people doing" hunt.
- Every claim needed a dated source.
- Prices, 1-month and 3-month moves, earnings dates, consensus and reported quarterly revenue
  were re-checked on TradingView (closes as of Oct 9 2026).

**Limits:**
- The research passes could not open primary pages; the build proxy blocked them. Their figures
  come from search-result summaries of the URLs listed.
- Check the primary page before relying on any number.
- These are research leads, not trade recommendations. Nothing here was traded.

**Grading:** each idea is graded after its report. Right, wrong, or right for the wrong reason
all count.

### 1. YUM (Taco Bell): negative into Nov 3

**Ground data:**
- Placer.ai: Taco Bell visits from Jul 6 to Sep 11 were 12.2% below last year, even with $1
  Enchiritos and $1 Mexican Pizzas running.
- The week of Jul 13 was about −19%.
- Sources: Restaurant Dive Sep 17; Restaurant Business Sep 21 ("traffic still down double digits
  despite aggressive promotions"); Entrepreneur Sep 23.
- Discounting that deep while traffic still falls is the tell.

**What management says:**
- Q2 call (Jul 30): Taco Bell US quarter-to-date comps were −2% through Jul 27 and "already
  improving".
- Barclays conference (Sep 9): "on plan", "several days with positive sales".
- Taco Bell is about 43% of divisional operating profit, excluding Pizza Hut.

**Consensus (TradingView):**
- Q3 revenue $2.114B, up 6.8% from $1.979B in Q3 2025. Q2 2026 grew 12.2%.
- Q3 EPS $1.54 vs. $1.41.
- 30 analysts: 14 Buy or Overweight, 16 Hold, 0 Sell. Average target $171.92, 18.7% above the
  price.

**Price:** $144.82, −1.9% over 1 month, −11.4% over 3 months. Only Guggenheim ($155) and TD Cowen
have trimmed targets.

**Why the market may be missing it:** the numbers point the other way from management, and nobody
has a Sell.

**What proves it wrong:**
- Visits are not sales; delivery and digital orders may differ.
- A Taco Bell US Q3 comp of about flat or better kills it.
- The Placer data is public, so part of it may already be priced.

### 2. HOOD: positive into Oct 27

**Ground data:** the company's own monthly operating data.

| Line | Q3 so far (Jul, Aug, Sep 1–24) | Q2 total | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Options contracts | 851M | 774M | +10% |
| Event contracts | 15.2B | 13.6B | +12% |
| Crypto notional | $45.4B | about $40B | +13% |
| Equity notional | $935B | $956B | −2%, with 4 trading days still missing |

The margin book was a record $21.6B.

**Consensus (TradingView):**
- Q3 revenue $1.418B, only 5.7% above Q2 2026's $1.341B. Q2 itself grew 34.5% year over year.
- Q3 EPS $0.55.

**Price:** $109.02, −9.7% over 1 month. It fell with Bitcoin (back to about $83K) and on
CEO and general counsel stock sales of about $43.8M. Those are price- and flow-driven, not
activity-driven.

**What proves it wrong:**
- Lower revenue per contract or per trade, especially in event contracts as Kalshi's rivals add
  pressure.
- A soft October or Q4 outlook.
- The sell-side models these monthly numbers too, so the gap may be smaller than it looks.

### 3. FLUT over DKNG: FanDuel taking share

**Ground data:**
- Wedbush (Oct 8): through NFL Week 4, DraftKings' average weekly daily active users were down
  about 7% year over year. FanDuel's were up about 9%.
- New York weekly reports:

  | NFL week | DraftKings handle | DraftKings share |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | $216.7M | 36.4% |
  | 2 | $208.4M | 34.1% |
  | 3 | $196.2M | 34.2% |

  FanDuel took $221–238M a week.

**A correction to the first read:**
- DKNG's Q3 consensus of $1.407B looks like +23% year over year. But Q3 2025 ($1.144B) was a
  bad-hold quarter, when bet outcomes favored customers.
- Against Q2 2026 ($1.443B) it is about flat, which is normal seasonality. So the case is share
  loss, not an absurd consensus.

**Price:** both stocks are near multi-year lows: DKNG −43% and FLUT −63% year to date. That makes
a relative (pair) view the cleaner expression than a short.

**What proves it wrong:** normal hold rescues DKNG's quarter, or FanDuel Predicts cannibalizes
FanDuel's own sportsbook.

### 4. QSR (Burger King): positive into Oct 29; the other side of #1

**Ground data:**
- Placer: Burger King grew visits Jul 6–Sep 11, while McDonald's, Wendy's and Subway declined.
  Dragon Ball Super week: +20.1%, flattered by the Labor Day shift.
- The QSR category fell 2.2% in September, its 7th straight monthly decline.
- Burger King US Q2 comps were +8.5%.

**Consensus:** Q3 revenue $2.538B, +3.6% year over year.

**Price:** $70.95, −9.2% over 1 month. It is being sold with the burger category, and three
brokers trimmed targets since Sep 25.

**What proves it wrong:** Burger King US is only part of RBI. A soft print at Tim Hortons Canada,
Popeyes or International swamps it.

### 5. HLT: positive into Oct 27

**Ground data (CoStar/STR, US hotels):**
- July RevPAR +8.2%, August +2.0%, September month-to-date +11.2%.
- Weekly: +10.4%, +14.2% and +12.9% through the week of Oct 3, double digits in 4 of the last 5
  weeks.
- CoStar raised its 2026 US outlook to +4.4% from +2.8%.

**Guidance:** Hilton guided Q3 systemwide RevPAR about +4%.

**Consensus:** Q3 revenue $3.37B (+7.9% year over year), EPS $2.35.

**Price:** $326.56, +8.2% over 1 month. MAR is +12.1% over 1 month, so part of this is already
priced.

**What proves it wrong:**
- Holiday calendar shifts, which CoStar flags itself.
- International and Las Vegas weakness (Las Vegas RevPAR −17.6% in the week of Oct 3).
- A cautious Q4 outlook around the midterms.

## Watch list (thinner evidence)

- **WGO (Oct 21):** August RV shipments −16.4% and KeyBanc's US retail-registration estimate
  −19.1%, against FY27 EPS consensus up about 32%. The $661M cap and heavy de-rating mean a sharp
  move on any print.
- **VFC (Oct 28):** StockX first half of 2026: Vans trades +238%, resale premium from +12% to
  +69%. Against that, Vans revenue fell 9% last quarter. Resale heat is a leading signal, but a
  tiny share of revenue.
- **DUOL (Nov 3):** an Aug 18 Form 8-K disclosed about 27.4% daily-active-user growth after a slide
  was shown by accident (Q2 was 23%). DA Davidson's panel shows 26–28% for Q3. Rated Hold on
  average.
- **SG (Nov 5):** the guide assumed a 600–700 basis-point cyclospora hit to Q3 comps, while fast
  casual held up in Placer data. But the stock is already +39% over 1 month.
- **MAT (Oct 27):** this is the first holiday with KPop Demon Hunters toys on shelves. How much of
  sales they are is unknown, and the stock is moving on deal news.

## Checked and rejected

- **TPB:** nicotine-pouch share gains are real, but they are already in its own revenue (+23% year
  over year). EBITDA is falling 28–40% year over year, so the stock is pricing real margins, not
  missing a trend.
- **TXRH:** the TikTok-hoax and traffic signals are contested; StoneX and management disagree.
- **CVNA, KMX:** the Manheim used-car price index is widely watched, and its effect on them is
  ambiguous.
- **Airlines:** fuel prices are fully public.
- **GME:** the move is driven by Ryan Cohen's buying, and the stock is +39% over 1 month.
- **Already priced in:** IMAX, CNK, AMC, ELF, ONON, SN, MCD, WEN, PZZA, CELH, COIN, U, NFLX.
- **No fresh data:** DPZ (reports Oct 13), WING, CAVA, EAT, CAKE.
- **BOOT:** the only evidence is management's own.
- **ANF:** Q2 earnings quality is inflated by a tariff refund. Watch it, but there is no call.
