# Where market-moving news broke first (June to October 2026)

*Research compiled 2026-10-09 for the "Bobby Axelrod" goal: hear it before everyone else does.*

**What we did:** took ~58 stories that moved stocks between mid-June and Oct 9, 2026 and traced each back to the **earliest public place it appeared**. Then we added those places to News247 as sources it checks first, and added each first report's wording to the backtest (`news247/data/history.yaml`).

**Limits:**
- The research sandbox could not open most original articles; its proxy blocked them.
- Timings and first sources therefore come from dated secondary coverage.
- Reconstructed headlines are marked `approx: true` in the history file.
- "First source" means the earliest one we could identify, not a proven first.
- `[unverified]` marks anything we could not confirm.

**Timing patterns:**
- **Weekend first sources hit hardest.**
  - The Amodei essay and Boeing's statement came out on Saturdays.
  - The FT's AstraZeneca–BMS story and Pulte's post came out on Sunday.
  - Asian proxies (SoftBank, Kospi) and futures moved before US cash.
- **Intraday scoops** (Reuters/Workday, WSJ/UGI, Bloomberg/Varonis) caused volatility halts within minutes. TV write-ups trailed by 5–30 minutes.
- **Filings beat press releases.** Generac's 8-K came ~1 day earlier. Nvidia's 13G landed after hours and the stock moved the next morning.
- **The call, not the release:** Nvidia's direction on Aug 26 came from the CFO's guidance on the call.
- **Trump's posts are now sold early.** The paid Truth API (Aug 1, 2026; $60–100K/month) gives subscribers posts "milliseconds" early. Every free feed is structurally behind them, so treat a Trump headline as partly priced in by the time any free feed shows it.

**If a page changes:** a page-watch source that matches no links now reports itself as **failing** on the dashboard instead of silently finding nothing. Run `news247 check` from the server to test every source.

**Skipped on purpose:**
- Crypto exchange listing feeds (Binance, Upbit): they don't move stocks.
- NYSE/Cboe halt pages: the Nasdaq halts feed already covers all US listings.
- Benzinga section RSS: retired. The Alpaca websocket is better.
- CourtListener's API: free limits dropped to 5/min and 125/day in 2026. Its email alerts or PACER RSS work instead.
- openFDA CRLs: publishing paused or disputed in 2026.

## 4. What the scorer learned

Of the 54 newly added events, the old criteria caught only ~24 from the first report. New entities, phrases and themes in `news247/data/knowledge.yaml`:

| Theme | What it catches | 2026 example |
|---|---|---|
| `deal_scoop` | "in talks / held talks / said to weigh a bid / explored / hired bankers" + takeover nouns | Workday, UGI, Varonis, AZ–BMS, Chipotle |
| `deal_collapse` | "abandon / walk away / never was a deal / no discussions" | PayPal −15%, AZ–BMS reversal |
| `housing_finance_regulator` | FHFA/Pulte/Fannie/Freddie + VantageScore, pricing grid, "hereby", "effective immediately" | FICO −17% and −26% |
| `yield_threshold` | "highest since 2007/2002" on Treasury yields | Aug 18, Sep 30 |
| `asia_market_shock` | Kospi/Nikkei/SK Hynix/SoftBank crash or surge | Jul 28 |
| `fda_action`, `trial_endpoint` | briefing documents, CRLs, "did not meet / met its primary endpoint" | CAPR, UNCY, RARE, MRNA |
| `court_ruling`, `sanctions_action` | SCOTUS/CIT/CAFC/judges on tariffs or big tech; OFAC/BIS/Section 232 | IEEPA, Google remedies |
| `index_inclusion` | "set to join S&P 500" | Ferguson |
| `prediction_market_jump` | Polymarket/Kalshi odds jumps | Iran ceasefire bets |
| `strategic_stake` | 13D/13G by a strategic or activist holder (passive Vanguard/BlackRock 13Gs don't count) | Nvidia–Nebius |
| `government_financing`, `congress_china_finding`, `ceo_exit_now`, `regulator_blocks_product`, `crypto_legislation` | DoD loans, China-committee findings, "resigns effective immediately", FAA delays, CLARITY Act | WOLF, BULL, MDB, BA, COIN |

**Also changed:**
- The FDA, FAA and FHFA are not counted as "market-wide authorities"; they move single stocks.
- A small, untracked company's news stays scaled down unless a sector theme names its tickers. An unknown biotech's FDA approval stays MEDIUM.

**Backtest (`news247 backtest`):**
- **95% of 151 events caught from the first report**, up from 76% on the enlarged set.
- **0 of 103 look-alike noise headlines alerted.**
- The new noise includes routine Federal Register paperwork, passive 13G filings, Pulte's meeting posts, routine CENTCOM operations and flat Polymarket markets.
- **Still missed, honestly:**
  - J.B. Hunt's profit warning (51.5, just under the line).
  - A few older hard cases.
