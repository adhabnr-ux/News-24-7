## What made AI and company news move stocks
1. **Who announced it.** In 2026 a product post from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google could move a whole sector 5–25%. So could a top-tier scoop (WSJ, FT, The Information, Bloomberg) on OpenAI's finances or deals.
2. **What kind of product.** Agents, plugins and apps that do a *named job* hit hardest: legal review, insurance comparison, tax planning, COBOL migration, code security, customer support, e-signature. Generic model upgrades mattered less.
3. **An identifiable incumbent with seat or fee exposure.** TRI/RELX (legal), WTW (brokers), IBM (COBOL), CRWD (security), DOCU, HUBS/CRM/WDAY.
4. **Size of the money relative to the company.** AMD +24% on a 6 GW deal, while NVDA rose only +4% on $100B.
5. **Surprise vs. a stated expectation.** 22.7% vs 25%, $50B vs $70B, $11B vs $7.15B.
6. **Regime.** After Feb 3, 2026, even small catalysts (Altruist, Insurify, a leak) caused big moves.
7. **Scoops vs. denials.** Official denials limit the damage but rarely reverse it the same day.
8. **Timing.** After-hours 8-Ks and earnings gap at the open. Friday and weekend launches show up Monday. DeepSeek and Cowork took days to hit stocks, so News247 texts lab posts immediately rather than waiting for price action.
