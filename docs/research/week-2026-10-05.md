## Before: the rules as they were on Oct 11

Only **1 of 7** first headlines would have pushed (PCVX). Four separate gaps:

1. **Takeover targets weren't recognized when the buyer is named first.**
   - "C.H. Robinson to buy RXO for $5.8 billion" scored 18 (LOW) and tagged nothing.
   - "Schneider Electric to buy PTC…" scored 16.
   - The only takeover rule read "X to be acquired" phrasing, and only for companies under $10B.
2. **Companies whose name is their ticker were invisible.**
   - PTC, RXO and XP have names under 4 letters, which the name index skipped.
   - Even "PTC to Be Acquired by Schneider Electric for $205 Per Share" tagged no ticker.
3. **Medicare star ratings weren't a rule.**
   - "4-star or higher plans" matched no term.
   - The Reuters headline scored 15, and CMS's own page title scored 20.
4. **Outbreak scares and emerging-market elections weren't rules.**
   - "Plague" was not an outbreak word, and Brazil's election was not an election.
   - Their headlines tagged oil and defense stocks (from "Russia") instead of the vaccine
     makers or the Brazilian ADRs.

The radar had a fifth gap. The pre-market and after-hours price scan read only the 400 largest
companies, so RXO, OPCH and XP were invisible outside the regular session.

## After: what changed

- **A deal reader** (`analysis/deals.py`) for any listed company. It reads four shapes:
  - "X to buy / to acquire / near deal to buy / in talks to buy Y";
  - "Y to be acquired (for $P) by X";
  - "X and Z sign agreement to acquire Y";
  - "Y to merge with X" (the smaller company is the target).

  It resolves the target by full name, a ticker-named company, the first words of one name
  ("Option Care" → Option Care Health) or a capitalized ticker. The target is tagged first and
  scores up: +40 for a definitive deal, +30 for talks, +5 more for a 20%+ premium.

  It also avoids false takeovers:
  - **Not deals:** questions, buybacks, stakes, units, asset purchases, "reasons to buy",
    "rush to buy", and an investor buying "$X of" a stock.
  - **Small purchases by a giant:** when a giant buys something worth under 2% of its own
    value, the deal words no longer count for the buyer.
- **New rules:**
  - `medicare_star_ratings`;
  - `pandemic_shock`, now covering plague, Ebola, H5N1, mpox and Marburg, and tagging vaccine
    and biodefense stocks;
  - `brazil_election` and `argentina_election`;
  - penalties for earnings previews ("ahead of earnings", "what to expect").
- **New sources:** `gnews-outbreaks` (2 min) and `gnews-em-elections` (5 min).
- **The radar rotates** through every other $2B+ company, 200 per sweep, on top of the largest
  400. Every $2B+ company is seen before the open and after the close within ~6 minutes.

## What this does not prove

- **The lags are modeled, not measured.** They are polling intervals; publishers' own feed
  delays add to them (`news247 stats` measures them live).
- **The first trace is TradingView's.** A wire or X post may have come earlier. The scorecard
  measures this live from now on: `news247 scorecard`.
- **Pre-market times rest on the radar's schedule.** The radar reads every minute from 04:00
  ET, so PTC and XP are "the first minute the radar could see the move". Their exact first
  pre-market trade isn't in the regular-session bars.
