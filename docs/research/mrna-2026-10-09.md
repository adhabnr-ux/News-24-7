**Fixes, all in place now:**

1. **The radar covers every size.** Each band has its own thresholds:
   - under $2B: 20%
   - $2–10B: 10%
   - $10–200B: 6%
   - $200B+: 4%

   A stock flags again at each higher rung, and pushes at 1.5× the band threshold.
   - **Pre-market and after-hours sweep:** a Yahoo spark sweep covers the 400 largest companies
     every minute, because the screeners only rank the regular session.
   - **Regular session:** Yahoo's day gainers and losers are scanned too.
   - **Liquidity:** companies of $2B+ need no dollar-volume figure.
   - **Restarts:** the radar's daily state is saved to disk, so a restart neither repeats an
     alert nor misses a stock that was already moving. The old silent start-up baseline is gone.

   **Result:** MRNA +9.2% pre-market on Oct 9 is a HIGH push.
2. **Index changes go on the calendar.** "Joins the Nasdaq-100 / S&P 500 … effective prior to
   market open on Friday, October 9" becomes "MRNA joins the Nasdaq-100 (effective at the
   open)" at 09:30. The morning Brief says "Tomorrow …" the day before and "Today …" on the day.
   Radar alerts cite it: "On the calendar today: …".
3. **Index announcements are scored.** Nasdaq-100 additions now carry the same keywords as S&P 500
   additions: 69, HIGH. The Google News index query includes the Nasdaq-100.
4. **New health-policy themes.** `vaccine_policy_push` and `vaccine_policy_pullback` cover
   agencies such as the NIH, NCI, HHS, CDC, BARDA and FDA acting on vaccines or mRNA, with
   tickers MRNA, BNTX, NVAX and PFE. The NIH, NCI, HHS, CDC, BARDA and CMS are now known entities.
   - The NYT report scores 55 (HIGH).
   - An HHS cancellation from the agency itself scores 60 (HIGH, down).
   - Routine agency news stays LOW.
5. **Health sources.** New feeds: NYT Health, STAT, Endpoints, Fierce Biotech, BioPharma Dive,
   and a Google News health-policy query. The scoop query now includes nytimes.com, statnews.com
   and axios.com.
6. **Dates for companies of any size.** Readout, PDUFA, panel, medical-meeting and index dates
   resolve the subject company: the wire's tag, "(Nasdaq: MRNA)", or its name. The index
   provider is never mistaken for the subject.
7. **Listed companies of $2B+ are never "untracked tickers".**

**Regression.** `tests/test_mrna_gaps.py` replays every link of the chain. The backtest gains
two events (`moderna-nasdaq-100`, `moderna-nih-cancer-vaccines`) and three health-agency
noise lines.
