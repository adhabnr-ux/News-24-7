# Post-mortem: Crown Castle (CCI) +15.6%, Friday Oct 9 2026

**What happened.** On Thursday Oct 8, after the close, SpaceX agreed to buy Grain Management's
nationwide 800 MHz spectrum. The Wall Street Journal put the price at about $8 billion; the deal
lets SpaceX run a standalone Starlink mobile service, pending FCC approval. The US carriers fell in
extended trading. On Friday:

The towers rose because SpaceX would need ground sites for the new spectrum; the carriers fell on a

**Fixes, all in place now:**

1. **A rule for satellite spectrum deals.** `satellite_carrier_threat` covers SpaceX or Starlink
   buying spectrum, licenses or a mobile-carrier business. It tags VZ, T and TMUS as "down".
   `satellite_tower_readthrough` adds CCI, AMT and SBAC as "up".
   - The Reuters headline now scores 93 (CRITICAL), the WSJ one 58 (HIGH), and Benzinga's CCI
     headline 72 (HIGH).
   - Routine SpaceX news (launches, subscriber counts, a new country for direct-to-cell texting)
     stays LOW.
2. **Deal words follow the rule's direction.** "SpaceX *to acquire* …" used to read as "up"
   because "to acquire" is bullish for a takeover target. When the buyer is the subject and the
   tagged stocks are third parties, the rule's own direction wins: "mixed" here, carriers down and
   towers up.
3. **Named companies are tagged.** Any listed company worth $2B or more that a headline names is
   tagged, matched against Nasdaq's full company list. "Crown Castle", "American Tower",
   "Verizon", "AT&T" and "T-Mobile" all resolve now. This also lets a radar alert on CCI link the
   headline as its likely catalyst.
   - Listed names with a bracketed suffix ("American Tower Corporation (REIT)") now match.
   - Hyphenated names ("T-Mobile") now match.
4. **The radar's push threshold is a rung.** A $10–200B company now pushes the moment it crosses
   9%; AMT's +9.3% pushes.

Four gaps found and fixed in the replay:
1. **Company-wide surge.** Most of CCI's chain traded below 3× its own open interest. The tape
   now also compares a company's whole option volume with the previous session.
2. **No previous close.** A contract that never traded before couldn't register as a spike. It
   is now measured from its model value.
3. **Overnight news.** News after the close only kept its companies on the fast-read list for 2
   hours, which ran out before the open. It now lasts until two hours into the next session, and
   morning alerts cite last night's news.
4. **Push budget.** One company climbing through every step could take most of the 12 daily
   pushes. Now: one spike push per company per day (again at 10×), and one push per company per
   scan.

**Morning Brief.** The Brief at 08:15 ET lists the overnight news alerts. With the Reuters
headline at CRITICAL, Friday's Brief would have led with it, tagging the carriers and the towers.
