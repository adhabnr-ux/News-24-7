# Run News247 24/7 in the cloud and get iMessages from your own Mac relay (about 15 minutes)

**What you end up with:**
- News247 runs around the clock on a small cloud server (about $7/month), so your own computer can be off.
- It texts important market news to **your phone number as iMessages**. A Mac you control does the sending through News247's built-in relay. No Sendblue, no third-party service.
- While that Mac is asleep, alerts arrive as free push notifications instead. When it wakes, you get what you missed.

**You need:** a GitHub account, a credit card for the cloud host, your iPhone, and **a Mac signed in to Messages** (yours, a spare one, or a family member's; see [IMESSAGE-RELAY.md](IMESSAGE-RELAY.md#which-mac)).

---

## Step 1: Deploy the monitor to Render (5 min)

1. Deploy as in [SETUP-CLOUD.md, step 1](SETUP-CLOUD.md#step-1-deploy-to-render-5-min). Render asks for `WHATSAPP_TO` (your number) and `CONTACT_EMAIL`.
2. In Render → **news247** → **Environment**, add `RELAY_ENABLED=true` and `IMESSAGE_TO=<your number>`, e.g. `+15551234567`. To use only iMessage, also set `WHATSAPP_ENABLED=false`. Save; Render restarts the service.

The relay secret (`RELAY_SECRET`) and the dashboard password (`DASHBOARD_TOKEN`) are generated by Render.

## Step 2: Open your Setup page (1 min)

1. In Render, open the **news247** service → **Environment**, and copy the value of `DASHBOARD_TOKEN`.
2. Open your service's URL with the token added:
   `https://news247-xxxx.onrender.com/setup?token=PASTE_TOKEN_HERE`
3. Check that your number is shown. You can change it here at any time.

## Step 3: Turn a Mac into your iMessage sender (5 min)

1. **Recommended:** on that Mac, sign Messages into a **separate Apple ID**, not the one on your iPhone (Messages → Settings → iMessage).
   - Texts from your own Apple ID arrive silently as "notes to self".
   - A free new Apple ID works. Save it as a contact on your iPhone.
2. On the Setup page, under **Your iMessage relay**, press **Copy**.
3. On the Mac, open **Terminal**, paste, and press Enter. The installer sets everything up as a background service and runs a checklist.
4. If macOS asks whether **python** may control **Messages**, click **OK**.
5. *(Optional, recommended)* Follow the installer's Full Disk Access tip. It enables delivery receipts and text commands.
6. Back on the Setup page, the Mac now shows as connected. Press **Send test message**. Your iPhone should get an iMessage: "News247 test: alerts will arrive here".

Bookmark `https://news247-xxxx.onrender.com/?token=…`. It's your live dashboard (alerts, live news, market movers, source health), and it works on your phone too.

## Step 4: A backup for when the Mac sleeps (2 min, free, recommended)

1. Install the **ntfy** app on your iPhone. Subscribe to a hard-to-guess topic name, e.g. `news247-k7f3q9x2`.
2. In Render → **Environment**, add `NTFY_ENABLED=true`, `NTFY_BACKUP=true`, `NTFY_TOPIC=news247-k7f3q9x2`, and save.

Now an alert goes out as an iMessage when the Mac is awake. If the Mac doesn't answer within 5 seconds, it's pushed through ntfy instead, and it is **not** repeated as a late iMessage.

## Control it by text

Reply to any alert:

| Text | Effect |
|---|---|
| `PAUSE 2h` | Pause texts for a set time (`30m`, `1d`, …) |
| `STOP` | Pause until `RESUME` |
| `RESUME` | Texts back on |
| `CRITICAL` | Only the biggest alerts |
| `MORE` | Medium alerts too |
| `NORMAL` | Back to the default |
| `STATUS` | Is everything running? |

## If something doesn't work
The Setup page shows the exact error and the relay's state. See [IMESSAGE-RELAY.md → Troubleshooting](IMESSAGE-RELAY.md#troubleshooting).

## No Mac at all?
Use a paid service that runs the Macs for you, and switch the relay off (`RELAY_ENABLED=false`):

- **Sendblue:** free sandbox for verified contacts. Text your Sendblue number once from your iPhone. Its pricing page is ambiguous about outbound messages on the free plan, so test it. Set `SENDBLUE_ENABLED=true`, `SENDBLUE_API_KEY_ID`, `SENDBLUE_API_SECRET`.
- **Blooio:** ~$39/month. Set `BLOOIO_ENABLED=true`, `BLOOIO_API_KEY`.
- **Plain SMS (green bubble):** Textbelt. Set `TEXTBELT_ENABLED=true`, `TEXTBELT_KEY`.

## Other hosts
- **Railway** (~$5/month): New Project → Deploy from GitHub repo → add the same variables under **Variables** (`RELAY_ENABLED=true`, `IMESSAGE_TO`, `CONTACT_EMAIL`, `DASHBOARD_TOKEN`) → add a **Volume** mounted at `/data/data` → Settings → Networking → Generate Domain.
- **Fly.io** (~$3–4/month, needs its command-line tool): see the comments at the top of `fly.toml`.
- **Everything on one Mac** (free, but the Mac must stay on): `./deploy/install-macos.sh`.
