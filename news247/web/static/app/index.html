Unlock Foretape

Paste your access key (DASHBOARD_TOKEN in Render → Environment). You only do this once.

Put Foretape on your home screen

iPhone only delivers alerts to apps on the home screen. Three taps:

  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add
  3. Open Foretape from your home screen and turn on alerts

Turn on alerts

Get market-moving news as a notification the moment it breaks, even when the app is closed.

Notifications are blocked

Turn them on in Settings → Notifications → Foretape (iPhone) or your browser's site settings, then reopen the app.
